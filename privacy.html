Privacy policy

Effective October 24, 2026

Trellis is a family app for chores, allowance, and savings. This policy explains, in plain language, what information Trellis keeps and how it is used. The short version: your family's data exists to run your family's Trellis, and for nothing else.

What we collect

  • A parent's email address, used to sign in with a one-time code. There are no passwords.
  • The family content you create: member nicknames and optional profile details, chores and when they were completed, allowance amounts you choose to track, savings goals, reminders, projects, and special days. Trellis never connects to bank accounts and never moves real money; allowance in Trellis is a family scoreboard that parents settle outside the app.
  • Optional photos a parent adds for profiles or the home screen. These are stored in private storage and are visible only to your family.
  • Optional calendar events. If a parent connects a calendar by its private address, Trellis reads upcoming events to show your family's week. Trellis never changes your calendar, and the private address is visible only to parent accounts.

Children

Kids never get accounts in Trellis. There is no email, phone number, or password for a child. A child's profile is created and controlled by their parent and carries only a nickname, an age band, an avatar color, and an optional photo the parent adds. Parents can edit or delete a child's profile at any time. Screens made for kids contain no advertising and no third-party trackers.

Voice and AI features

When you use the chore setup assistant, the words you speak or type are sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to be turned into a structured chore list, along with first names or nicknames of family members so chores land with the right person. These requests are not used to train AI models.

What we never do

  • No advertising, and no advertising or analytics trackers from third parties.
  • We never sell your data, and we never share it for marketing.
  • We never collect more about a child than the profile a parent creates.

Where your data lives

Trellis stores data with Supabase in the United States. Every family's data is isolated by database-level security rules, so one family can never see another family's information, and a child's device sees only a child's view of their own family.

Your choices

You can edit or delete chores, goals, profiles, photos, and the calendar connection inside the app at any time. To delete your entire family and account, email hello@trellisfamily.app and we will remove it.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the date above will change and the app will say so. Questions are always welcome at hello@trellisfamily.app.
